Optical Link V1

Experimental visible-light data transfer using an ordinary screen as the transmitter and an ordinary camera as the receiver. V1 intentionally runs slowly while we validate geometry, colour calibration and packet integrity.

1. Sender

Enter text, generate C16 optical frames, switch to fullscreen, then play the packet sequence at 0.5–2 fps.

Open Sender

2. Receiver

Start the camera, point it at the fullscreen sender, and watch the receiver lock the ROI, decode colour symbols and reconstruct the transfer.

Open Receiver

Baseline profile: V1-G16-C16 · 256×256 logical ROI · 16×16 cells · 16 colour regions · CRC32 packet validation.
